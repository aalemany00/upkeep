Calendar

Imported calendars

Paste a calendar's iCal/webcal feed URL — Google Calendar, Outlook, and Apple Calendar all have one under their sharing/export settings. Fetched through a public CORS relay since calendar providers don't allow direct browser fetches, so your feed URL is sent to one of a few third-party relay services on every sync — avoid pasting a feed URL you consider sensitive. One-way: reads events in, never writes back to the source calendar. Recurring events currently show only their first occurrence.

          Diet auto-estimate setup (optional)

          The Food tab can suggest calories/protein when you type a food name, using the free USDA FoodData Central database. It needs a one-time, no-credit-card API key:

          1. Go to fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-key-signup and fill in the short form (just name/email, no card).
          2. Check your email for the key — it looks like a long string of letters and numbers.
          3. Open usda-config.js in the project and replace YOUR_API_KEY_HERE with your key.
          4. Save, redeploy (or just reload if running locally) — the Food tab will start suggesting estimates as you type.

          Without this, manual food logging still works exactly the same — you just type calories/protein in yourself.
